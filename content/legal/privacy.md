# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Rinku collects, how we use it,
and the choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication
identifiers from our identity provider. When you build a profile, we store
the display name, bio, username, profile photograph, banner image, and the
links you add. When a visitor uses one of your public links, we record a
counter and a timestamp for that click.

We do not run third-party advertising trackers or sell your data.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use your information to operate the service: to render your public
profile, to count clicks and page views for your insights page, and to keep
your account secure.

## 3. Sharing

Public profile content (username, display name, bio, images, and links) is
visible to anyone with your public URL. Account details such as your email
address are not published. Aggregated or anonymized analytics may be used to
improve the product.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can archive links at any time. Deleting a link removes it permanently
from public view; deleting a profile or your entire account removes all of
its links, images, visit records, and click records. We keep data only as
long as needed to provide the service.

## 5. Your Choices

You can edit or remove your profile content at any time from the dashboard.
You can permanently delete your account and its data from your account
settings. If you use OAuth sign-in, you can revoke access at the provider.

## 6. Security

We rely on our hosting and authentication providers for transport security
(TLS) and credential storage, and we do not store raw passwords ourselves.
No method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## 7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy over time. Material changes will be
reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use after changes
means you accept the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact link on the
Rinku homepage.
